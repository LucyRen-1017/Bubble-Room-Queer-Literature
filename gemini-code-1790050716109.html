Direct Submissions

How to Submit Your Work

We accept works year-round without fee or deadline. Please read our guidelines before emailing us.

Poetry

Send up to 6 poems in a single file. Experimental, free-verse, or formal forms are all welcome.

Prose & Essays

Short fiction, flash prose, or personal hybrid essays up to 3,500 words per submission.

Single-Document Submission Protocol

Please bundle all writing pieces, a cover letter, and your third-person bio into ONE single Word document (.doc / .docx) or PDF file.

• Include: Cover Letter, Name or Pseudonym, Contact Email, Piece Title(s), Text, and Third-Person Bio.

• Pseudonyms / Pen Names are 100% welcomed for safety and privacy.

• Subject Line Format: [Genre] - [Title / Poems] - [Your Name]

Send to bubbleroomlove@outlook.com No submission fees. Always rolling.
About the Journal

Our Manifesto

Bubble Room Literature is an independent, underground sanctuary dedicated to publishing authentic queer writers, poets, and storytellers.

We value quiet vulnerability, wild experimentation, and raw honesty over sanitized academic standards. We operate as a continuous rolling press, curating works into cohesive issues as they bloom. We are currently working on our second edition.

Note: We are not a paying publication yet, but in the future, we hope to change that and fully support more queer artists.
